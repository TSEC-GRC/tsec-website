From Framework Requirements to Operating Controls

The objective is not simply to map frameworks. The objective is to create an operating environment where controls are owned, evidenced, monitored, tested, and continuously improved.

R

Requirements Mapping

Identify applicable regulatory, contractual, security, privacy, and governance requirements and establish their relationship to the enterprise control environment.

C

Control Rationalization

Consolidate overlapping requirements into common control objectives while preserving framework-specific obligations and testing requirements.

O

Ownership & Accountability

Assign control ownership, responsibility, frequency, evidence requirements, and escalation paths across the organization.

E

Evidence Management

Establish repeatable evidence collection and validation processes that support multiple assessments without unnecessary duplication.

T

Testing & Monitoring

Monitor control effectiveness, identify exceptions, track remediation, and maintain continuous readiness between formal assessments.

G

Governance & Reporting

Translate control performance and residual risk into executive dashboards, management reporting, and strategic decision support.
